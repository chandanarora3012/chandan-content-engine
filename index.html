Chandan Content Engine

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 26, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Chandan Content Engine ("the App") handles information when it is used to create, manage, and publish professional content to a user's LinkedIn account.

The App is intended for personal use by its owner and is designed to minimize the collection and retention of personal information.

1. Information We May Access

When you connect the App to LinkedIn, the App may access information permitted by the LinkedIn authorization you grant, which may include:

  • Basic LinkedIn member/profile information needed to identify the authenticated account.
  • Authorization information such as an OAuth access token required to perform permitted actions.
  • Permission to create and publish content on behalf of the authenticated LinkedIn member when the applicable LinkedIn permission has been granted.

The App does not request or store your LinkedIn password.

2. How Information Is Used

Information accessed through LinkedIn is used only to operate the App's intended functionality, including:

  • Connecting the authenticated LinkedIn account to the App.
  • Generating and managing professional content drafts.
  • Publishing approved content to the authenticated LinkedIn member's profile.
  • Maintaining the technical authorization required for the App to perform these actions.

The App does not use LinkedIn member information for unrelated advertising, profiling, or sale to third parties.

3. Content Generation and Third-Party Services

The App may use third-party technology services, such as content-generation or hosting services, to provide its functionality. Only information reasonably necessary for the requested function should be transmitted to such services.

The App is not intended to sell personal information or LinkedIn member information to third parties.

4. Data Storage and Security

Access credentials and OAuth tokens should be stored securely and must not be exposed publicly. The App is designed to avoid storing LinkedIn passwords.

Reasonable technical and organizational measures should be used to protect stored authorization information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

5. Data Retention

The App retains information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide its functionality. Draft content may be retained for content management purposes. OAuth credentials may be retained while the LinkedIn connection remains active.

You may revoke the App's LinkedIn authorization at any time through the applicable LinkedIn account or application controls. After authorization is revoked, the App should no longer use the revoked authorization.

6. Sharing of Information

Personal information is not sold or rented. Information may be processed by service providers only when reasonably necessary to operate the App, such as hosting, authentication, analytics, or content-generation infrastructure.

Information may also be disclosed where required by applicable law, regulation, legal process, or to protect the security and integrity of the App.

7. LinkedIn

LinkedIn is a separate service governed by LinkedIn's own terms and privacy practices. Your use of LinkedIn remains subject to LinkedIn's policies. The App only requests and uses the LinkedIn permissions that are necessary for its intended functionality and that you authorize.

8. Your Choices

You may:

  • Choose whether to connect your LinkedIn account to the App.
  • Revoke LinkedIn authorization for the App.
  • Delete locally stored drafts and App data under your control.
  • Stop using the App at any time.

9. Children's Privacy

The App is intended for professional use and is not directed toward children. The App does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes in the App, applicable requirements, or privacy practices. The updated version will be published at the same privacy-policy location with a revised "Last updated" date.

11. Contact

For privacy questions or requests relating to Chandan Content Engine, please contact the App owner using the contact information associated with the application or the account through which the App is operated.
